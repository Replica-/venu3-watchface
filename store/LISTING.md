# Connect IQ Store listing

## Name

Replica Watch Face

## Short description

A bold gradient watch face with your health stats, weather and live heart rate and stress graphs.

## Description

Replica puts everything you check most on one clean, glanceable screen, wrapped in a smooth colour gradient that you can make your own.

**At a glance**
- Large gradient time with AM/PM and seconds
- Date and battery level
- Weather, temperature and wind speed
- Current heart rate and calories burned
- Intensity minutes for the last 7 days, one block per day, split into moderate and vigorous
- Heart rate graph for the last 51 minutes
- Stress graph for the last 42 minutes

**Make it yours**
- Choose the gradient start and end colours
- Colour mode: your colours, a new colour pair every hour, or colours that follow the time of day, season and weather
- Show the graphs in coloured zones or in your gradient colours
- Tailor the heart rate colours to your own range, or use your Garmin heart rate zones
- Adjust the stress level bands

**Battery friendly**
- Most of the face is drawn once a minute; only the seconds update while you're looking at it
- A dim, minimal always-on display that shifts slightly every minute to protect the screen

Note: the graphs build up over time, so they may look empty when you first install the face.

To change the settings, open the Garmin Connect app and go to Device, Watch Faces, Replica Watch Face, Settings.
